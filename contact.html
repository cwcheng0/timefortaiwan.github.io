Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Booking With Us

How do I customize a private tour itinerary in Taiwan?

Simply submit the inquiry form above or email us directly at mike@timefortaiwan.com with your expected travel dates, preferred destinations (e.g. Taipei, Jiufen, Taroko Gorge, Sun Moon Lake, Alishan, Taitung), and group size. Our local specialists will propose a custom day-by-day itinerary with pricing.

Do you provide licensed English-speaking tour guides and private vans?

Yes! All our tour guides are licensed by the Taiwan Tourism Administration. We arrange comfortable air-conditioned 7-seater to 9-seater vans, mini-buses, or luxury coaches with professional drivers tailored to your group size.

How far in advance should we contact and book?

We recommend contacting us 2 to 6 weeks in advance, especially during peak seasons (Spring cherry blossoms, Autumn, and New Year holidays) or for cruise shore excursions, to guarantee guide and vehicle availability.

Can you accommodate special dietary needs (vegetarian, halal, gluten-free)?

Contact us with any dietary preferences, and we will select certified restaurants and customized meals accordingly. Taiwan is exceptionally friendly to vegetarian, vegan, and halal travelers.
