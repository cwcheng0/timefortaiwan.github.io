解構台灣靈魂的
法式美學饗宴

「態芮」為店名 Taïrroir 的中文諧音，為主廚 Kai Ho 和團隊深具創意堅持細節的態度，不論是創意或態度或手法，也如同春天新生芮草般，將不斷延伸發展。 而 Taïrroir，是一個清晰又富深意自創字， 以"Taiwan"（原文，台灣）與" Terroir"（法文，風土）融合而成，象徵著透過穿越國界疆域的嶄新思維，融合台灣料理本質，進行多元能量華美釋放，期待給賓客不同的感受，傳遞「在台灣發現世界，讓世界看見台灣」。在台北大直的靜謐空間中，以極致的法式烹飪技藝，重新詮釋台灣在地風土的溫暖記憶。

"Taïrroir" is a transliteration reflecting Chef Kai Ho and his team's creative attitude. A fusion of "Taiwan" and "Terroir" (French for terroir), it symbolizes a new way of thinking that transcends national boundaries to reinterpret the warm memories of Taiwan's local culture with exquisite French culinary techniques.

01

精緻開胃小點

Amuse-Bouche

饗宴的第一樂章。精緻的木製承架上呈現三款一口小點：Ceviche 金桔白魽塔，點綴上蘋果丁及辣椒凍； 芋頭粿，中間放上Tartar Sauce，上方為魚子醬及麵包丁； 雞肝夾心脆片，點綴青芒果。

The first movement of the feast. Three bite-sized treats are presented on an exquisite wooden stand: Ceviche, a kumquat tart topped with diced apple and chili jelly; taro cake, with tartar sauce in the center and topped with caviar and bread cubes; and chicken liver sandwich crisps, garnished with green mango.

金桔白魽塔 | Ceviche 芋頭粿 | Taro Cake 雞肝夾心脆片 | Chicken Liver Sandwich Crisps
02

金玉滿餃

Sweet Corn Velouté, Coconut, Coffee & Duo Crystal Ravioli

湯品「金玉滿餃」為玉米湯搭配椰奶泡泡，再點綴咖啡油，Side dish 是以雙層義大利餃 Doppio 為靈感，但我們是以水晶餃皮製作，內餡為奶油蘑菇菠菜、雞肉慕斯。 原菜單上的 Velouté 中文翻譯為天鵝絨醬，其法文原譯為「絲絨般的光滑」，因此以 Velouté 作為湯品滑順的意象。

The soup, "Golden Dumplings," is corn soup with coconut milk foam and garnished with coffee oil. The side dish is inspired by the double-layered Italian ravioli, but made with crystal dumpling wrappers filled with creamy mushroom, spinach, and chicken mousse. "Velouté" represents the velvet-like smoothness of the soup.

玉米湯 | Sweet Corn Velouté 椰奶泡泡 | Coconut Foam 咖啡油 | Coffee Oil 薄皮雙餃 | Handcrafted Ravioli
03

「迷你法國麵包」及「全麥胚芽麵包」

Artisanal Sourdough & Signature Butter

新鮮烘烤出爐的歐式麵包，外皮無比香脆，內裡氣孔飽滿、濕潤軟Q。搭配塑成精緻「T」字樣的半鹽奶油，純淨的乳香與麥香相得益彰。奶油是以苗栗四方牧場的鮮奶油自製的無鹽奶油(非半鹽奶油)，另外附上來自嘉義布袋的鹽之花。

Freshly baked artisanal sourdough with a golden crisp crust and an airy, moist, chewy interior. Served with signature butter shaped into the letter "T," made from fresh cream from Miaoli Sifang Ranch, accompanied by fleur de sel from Budai, Chiayi.

低溫發酵歐式麵包 | Artisanal Sourdough T字特製無鹽奶油 | Signature "T" Butter 嘉義布袋鹽之花 | Fleur de Sel
04

代表作「貝露知筍」

Hokkaido Scallop with Bamboo Shoots, Shellfish, Avocado & Lemon Verbena

搭以北海道生食級干貝，搭配上綠竹筍丁、酪梨丁、金桔美乃滋及馬鞭草油，上方以果凍狀呈現的為 Shellfish Jelly 及椰子汁 Jelly。 裝飾的花草為牙蔥。

Made with Hokkaido-grade raw scallops, topped with diced green bamboo shoots, diced avocado, kumquat mayonnaise, and verbena oil. Served with shellfish jelly and coconut milk jelly on top, garnished with shallots.

北海道扇貝 | Hokkaido Scallop 綠竹筍 | Green Bamboo Shoot 酪梨 | Avocado 金桔美乃滋 | Kumquat Mayonnaise 馬鞭草油 | Verbena Oil
05

傳奇代表作「休息站的回憶」

Pu'er Tea Free Range Organic Egg - Legendary Signature

「休息站的回憶」為主廚招牌菜。普洱茶溏心蛋搭配底部的 Fondant(方旦)地瓜、蘿蔔乾，上方為裸麥脆片，脆片上點綴酸豆、脆瓜、鹹檸檬、紅麴豆腐乳、甜豆泥，另外桌邊倒入雞湯白米粥。「Fondant」的意思為：用奶油及雞湯煎成外脆內軟的口感。靈感來自 Chef Kai 兒時與家人搭客運在台灣旅行，在休息站買熱呼呼的茶葉蛋的記憶。放牧紅殼蛋以普洱茶湯低溫浸泡成溏心蛋，搭配 57 號方旦地瓜煎製的 Fondant，再淋上雞湯熬煮的白米粥。

"Memories of the Rest Stop" is the chef's signature dish inspired by Chef Kai's childhood memories of buying hot tea eggs at bus rest stops. Free-range eggs are slow-cooked in Pu-erh tea, paired with No. 57 sweet potato fondant, dried radish, rye crisps with condiments, and finished with warm chicken broth rice porridge poured tableside.

方旦地瓜 | Sweet Potato Fondant 蕾絲薄餅 | Pain Dentelle 普洱茶溏心蛋 | Pu'er Tea Egg 雞湯白米粥 | Rice Congee
06

蘆獲至鮑

Panko Abalone Cutlet

靈感來自廣東菜的杏汁燉豬肺，使用南非鮑魚低溫慢煮再炸，搭配來自雲林的綠蘆筍、杏仁茶泡泡，上方點綴雞皮脆片，醬汁為 Chicken Jus.

Inspired by Cantonese cuisine, this dish features braised pork lung in almond sauce, slow-cooked and deep-fried South African abalone, green asparagus from Yunlin, almond tea bubbles, and crispy chicken skin with Chicken Jus.

杏汁燉豬肺 | Almond Juice Stewed Pork Lung 南非鮑魚 | South African Abalone 雲林綠蘆筍 | Green Asparagus 杏仁茶泡泡 | Almond Tea Bubbles
07

七巧魚瓏

Pan-Seared Seasonal Fish

乾煎每日鮮魚，魚底部為梅干菜肉餅，上方為五種脆片(芹菜、蔥、韭菜、蒜苗、香菇)，旁邊小點為七菜塔，醬汁為Choron Sauce (以Béarnaise為基底，加入紅糟及蜆製成的醬汁)

Pan-seared daily fresh fish set on a preserved vegetable and pork patty, topped with five crispy vegetable slices (celery, scallion, chive, garlic sprout, shiitake). Accompanied by a seven-vegetable tower and Choron Sauce (Béarnaise base with red yeast wine lees and clams).

每日鮮魚 | Seared Fish 梅干菜肉餅 | Pork & Preserved Veg Patty 五種脆片 | Five Crisps 七菜塔 | Seven Vegetable Tower 醬汁 | Choron Sauce
8

經典主菜：屏東振聲鴨三吃「鴨韻成章」

Main Course: Pingtung Duck in Three Ways

主廚向台灣經典烤鴨致敬的得意之作。 來自屏東振聲鴨的烤鴨胸，搭配洛神醃白玉苦瓜薄片及鴨腿肉香腸，旁邊點綴的是洛神花粉、糯米椒油及糯米椒 Purée，醬汁為 Duck Jus。 Second Service：使用來自石碇的許家手工麵線，放上酸菜及鴨腿肉、青花椒油，由服務人員桌邊倒入酸菜鴨肉湯。 鴨肉推薦5分熟。

Chef Kai's homage to classic Taiwanese roast duck. Roasted duck breast from Zhen Sheng Duck in Pingtung served with pickled white bitter melon, duck leg sausage, roselle pollen, shishito chili oil/purée, and Duck Jus. Second Service: Handmade noodles from Xu Family in Shiding served with sauerkraut, duck meat, Sichuan peppercorn oil, and tableside-poured duck broth.

屏東振聲熟成烤鴨 | Aged Pingtung Duck 白芝麻脆皮 | Sesame Crust 石碇許家手工麵線 | Handmade Shiding Noodles 芙蓉洛神花漬苦瓜 | Pickled Roselle Bitter Melon

第一吃：芝麻脆皮鴨胸與鴨肉卷

芝麻煮鴨皮下，是無比粉嫩多汁的熟成鴨胸肉；搭配鹹香的手工鴨肉丸卷與精美的洛神花苦瓜玫瑰。

Crispy golden sesame skin over tender aged duck breast, paired with handcrafted savory duck roll and pickled hibiscus bitter melon rose.

第二吃：鴨油酸菜細粉湯（石碇許家手工麵線）

清亮而濃郁的鵝澄清湯帶著酸菜的爽口與發酵酸香，細粉吸飽了湯汁，溫暖脾胃；麵線是來自石碇的許家手工麵線。

Rich duck broth balanced with fermented sauerkraut, duck leg meat, green Sichuan peppercorn oil, and handmade noodles from Shiding.

前甜點「仙姿玉質」

Pre-Dessert: Xian Zi Yu Zhi

冰涼爽口的台式風味甜品重塑。底層是細嫩帶微辛的薑汁奶酪（薑汁撞奶），鋪上晶瑩剔透的蜂蜜檸檬冰沙，中央是一顆醇厚的仙草雪酪，最後托起一薄片香甜的黑糖蛋白霜，宛如仙子般優雅純淨。

A refreshing reimagining of classic Taiwanese dessert. Layered with ginger milk curd at the base, topped with honey lemon granita, grass jelly sorbet, and a delicate brown sugar meringue disc.

仙草雪酪 | Grass Jelly Sorbet 薑汁奶酪 | Ginger Milk Curd 檸檬蜂蜜冰沙 | Honey Lemon Granita 黑糖蛋白霜餅 | Brown Sugar Meringue

主甜點「鳳聲茶起」

Main Dessert: Feng Sheng Cha Qi

包種茶的醬汁無鮮奶油成份，以白巧克力做調和，呈現較濃稠滑順的質地。中間的冰品是鳳梨 Sorbet，桃紅色的部分為紅龍果慕斯，上方為鳳梨脆片。

The Baozhong tea sauce contains no heavy cream and is blended with white chocolate for a silky texture. The center features pineapple sorbet, dragon fruit mousse, and pineapple crisps.

The Soul of Taïrroir

與主廚相遇Chef Kai Ho 何順凱

「以法式烹調技法，解構台灣靈魂與風土。」

"Deconstructing Taiwanese soul and terroir through French culinary techniques."

主廚何順凱（Kai Ho）以敏銳的直覺與豐富的在地情感，將台灣傳統小吃、辦桌文化與家常菜記憶，利用當代法式精準技法重組。在他的帶領下，Taïrroir 態芮於 2023 年榮獲米其林三星肯定，成為全球首家獲得三星殊榮的現代台灣料理餐廳。

Chef Kai Ho leverages sharp intuition and local passion to deconstruct Taiwanese night market snacks, banquet dishes, and home-cooked memories using precise French techniques. Under his leadership, Taïrroir was awarded three Michelin stars in 2023, becoming the world's first modern Taiwanese restaurant to achieve 3 Michelin stars.
