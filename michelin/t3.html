解構台灣靈魂的法式美學饗宴

Deconstructing Taiwan's Soul: A French Aesthetic Feast

「態」代表態度，「芮」代表風土（Terroir）。在台北大直的靜謐空間中，以極致的法式烹飪技藝，重新詮釋台灣在地風土的溫暖記憶。

"Taï" represents attitude, while "rroïr" comes from "Terroir." Located in the serene area of Dazhi, Taipei, Taïrroir uses refined French culinary techniques to re-examine and elevate warm memories of local Taiwanese terroir.

經典饗宴時光軸 | Tasting Menu Timeline

01

精緻開胃小點

Amuse-Bouche

饗宴的第一樂章。精緻的木製承架上呈現三款一口小點：酥脆的小塔、如楓葉般輕薄的脆片，以及包裹著在地食材的圓餅，以脆、軟、滑三種層次喚醒味蕾。

The opening movement of the feast. Three bite-sized creations served on an elegant wooden frame—crispy mini tart, leaf-thin crisp, and a delicate wafer filled with local ingredients—awakening the palate with layers of crispness, tenderness, and smoothness.

寶島四季元素 | Taiwanese Seasons 法式脆塔 | French Tart 香草慕斯 | Herb Mousse

金玉滿餃

Sweet Corn Velouté & Delicate Ravioli

A silky velouté paired with handmade thin-wrapper ravioli, featuring a harmonious balance of coffee and coconut subtle sweet and tangy notes.

03

手作歐式麵包與專屬奶油

Artisanal Sourdough & Signature Butter

新鮮烘烤出爐的歐式麵包，外皮無比香脆，內裡氣孔飽滿、濕潤軟Q。搭配塑成精緻「T」字樣的半鹽奶油，純淨的乳香與麥香相得益彰。

Freshly baked artisanal sourdough with a golden crisp crust and an airy, moist, chewy interior. Served with signature semi-salted butter shaped into the letter "T," creating a delightful harmony of wheat and dairy aromas.

低溫發酵歐式麵包 | Artisanal Sourdough T字特製半鹽奶油 | Signature "T" Butter
04

代表作「貝露知筍」

Hokkaido Scallop with Bamboo & Lemon Verbena

搭以北海道扇貝，佐以綠竹筍，酪梨，搭配的裝飾是原住民排灣、魯凱與卑南族的馬鞭草，去除海鮮的腥味並加上台灣竹筍及水果而成的台式料理。

Featuring Hokkaido scallops paired with fresh green bamboo shoots and avocado, garnished with indigenous verbena herbs (Paiwan, Rukai, and Puyuma traditions) to complement the seafood with fresh citrusy notes and local fruits.

北海道扇貝 | Hokkaido Scallop 綠竹筍 | Green Bamboo Shoot 酪梨 | Avocado 馬鞭草 | Lemon Verbena
05

傳奇代表作「休息站的回憶-迷魂香芋椪糖」

Pu'er Tea Free Range Organic Egg - Legendary Signature

態芮最著名的靈魂代表作的變形。頂上是乾蓮藕雲朵，底層藏有溫潤的有機普洱茶葉蛋，表面鋪滿香脆的紫地瓜椪糖，口感在極致酥脆與溫潤絲滑間跳躍。

A signature variation of Taïrroir's iconic dish. Topped with a lotus root crisp cloud, revealing a warm organic Pu'er tea marble egg at the base, sprinkled with purple sweet potato honeycomb candy for a contrast of crispy and velvety textures.

紫地瓜椪糖 | Sweet Potato Honeycomb 有機普洱茶葉蛋 | Pu'er Tea Egg 乾蓮藕雲朵 | Lotus Root Cloud
06

經典主菜：屏東振聲鴨三吃「鴨韻成章」

Main Course: Pingtung Duck in Three Ways

主廚向台灣經典烤鴨致敬的得意之作。選用屏東振聲優質鴨肉，經過熟成與細緻烹調，完美呈現烤鴨胸的酥脆與多汁、鴨肉卷的醇厚，以及鴨油高湯搭配細粉的溫潤，成就一幅宏大的鴨肉樂章。

Chef Kai's homage to classic Taiwanese roasted duck. Premium Pingtung duck is aged and cooked to perfection, presenting crispy duck breast, savory duck rolls, and a comforting duck broth with vermicelli.

第一吃：芝麻脆皮鴨胸與鴨肉卷 | Course 1: Sesame Duck Breast & Roll

金黃香脆的芝麻烤鴨皮下，是無比粉嫩多汁的熟成鴨胸肉；搭配鹹香的手工鴨肉丸卷與精美的洛神花苦瓜玫瑰。

Crispy golden sesame skin over tender aged duck breast, paired with handcrafted savory duck roll and pickled hibiscus bitter melon rose.

第二吃：鴨油酸菜細粉湯 | Course 2: Duck Oil Sauerkraut Vermicelli Soup

清亮而濃郁的鴨湯帶著酸菜的爽口與發酵酸香，細粉吸飽了湯汁，溫暖脾胃。

Rich duck broth balanced with fermented sauerkraut and silky vermicelli absorbing every bit of flavor.

07

前甜點「仙姿玉質」

Pre-Dessert: Xian Zi Yu Zhi

冰涼爽口的台式風味甜品重塑。底層是細嫩帶微辛的薑汁奶酪（薑汁撞奶），鋪上晶瑩剔透的蜂蜜檸檬冰沙，中央是一顆醇厚的仙草雪酪，最後托起一薄片香甜的黑糖蛋白霜，宛如仙子般優雅純淨。

A refreshing reimagining of classic Taiwanese dessert. Layered with ginger milk curd at the base, topped with honey lemon granita, grass jelly sorbet, and a delicate brown sugar meringue disc.

仙草雪酪 | Grass Jelly Sorbet 薑汁奶酪 | Ginger Milk Curd 檸檬蜂蜜冰沙 | Honey Lemon Granita 黑糖蛋白霜餅 | Brown Sugar Meringue
08

主甜點「鳳聲茶起」

Main Dessert: Feng Sheng Cha Qi

將代表台灣靈魂的「鳳梨酥」與「高山茶」完美融合。以金黃酥香的鹹蛋黃費南雪做成圓環，填入香甜微酸的慢燉蜜漬鳳梨丁；置於溫潤清香的包種茶鮮奶油醬池中，搭配醇香的香草雪酪與酥脆的蜂巢太妃糖。

Merges iconic Taiwanese pineapple cake with high-mountain tea. A salted egg yolk financier ring filled with slow-simmered candied pineapple, resting in a pool of Baozhong tea cream sauce with vanilla sorbet and honeycomb toffee.

鹹蛋黃費南雪 | Salted Egg Financier 蜜漬鳳梨丁 | Candied Pineapple 包種茶茶醬 | Baozhong Tea Sauce 蜂巢太妃糖 | Honeycomb Toffee
09

茶點與茗心「飴意 / 茗心」

Mignardises Cart & Tea/Coffee

饗宴的優雅句點。態芮專屬的甜點推車緩緩駛至桌邊，承載著各式融合了法式精緻與台式童趣的手工小點，任由顧客挑選。再搭配一杯溫熱的精品茶或咖啡，以及清新芬芳的手工雪酪。

An elegant conclusion. The custom mignardises cart presents handcrafted treats blending French finesse with playful Taiwanese childhood nostalgia, served alongside premium tea or coffee and red guava sorbet.

Meet Chef Kai Ho (何順凱)

「以法式烹調技法，解構台灣靈魂與風土。」

"Deconstructing Taiwanese soul and terroir through French culinary techniques."

主廚何順凱（Kai Ho）以敏銳的直覺與豐富的在地情感，將台灣傳統小吃、辦桌文化與家常菜記憶，利用當代法式精準技法重組。在他的帶領下，Taïrroir 態芮於 2023 年榮獲米其林三星肯定，成為全球首家獲得三星殊榮的現代台灣料理餐廳。

Chef Kai Ho leverages sharp intuition and local passion to deconstruct Taiwanese night market snacks, banquet dishes, and home-cooked memories using precise French techniques. Under his leadership, Taïrroir was awarded three Michelin stars in 2023, becoming the world's first modern Taiwanese restaurant to achieve 3 Michelin stars.
