解構台灣靈魂的法式美學饗宴

Deconstructing Taiwan's Soul: A French Aesthetic Feast

「態芮」為店名 Taïrroir 的中文諧音，為主廚 Kai Ho 和團隊深具創意堅持細節的態度，不論是創意或態度或手法，也如同春天新生芮草般，將不斷延伸發展。 而 Taïrroir，是一個清晰又富深意自創字， 以"Taiwan"（原文，台灣）與" Terroir"（法文，風土）融合而成，象徵著透過穿越國界疆域的嶄新思維，融合台灣料理本質，進行多元能量華美釋放，期待給賓客不同的感受，傳遞「在台灣發現世界，讓世界看見台灣」。。在台北大直的靜謐空間中，以極致的法式烹飪技藝，重新詮釋台灣在地風土的溫暖記憶。

"泰芮" is a transliteration of the restaurant's name, Taïrroir, reflecting Chef Kai Ho and his team's creative and detail-oriented attitude. Their approach, whether in terms of creativity, attitude, or technique, is like the ever-growing iris in spring, constantly evolving and expanding. Taïrroir, a self-created word rich in meaning, is a fusion of "Taiwan" and "Terroir" (French for terroir or landscape), symbolizing a new way of thinking that transcends national boundaries. It represents the fusion of the essence of Taiwanese cuisine, releasing diverse energies in a magnificent way, hoping to offer guests a unique experience and conveying the message of "Discovering the world in Taiwan, letting the world see Taiwan." In the tranquil space of Dazhi, Taipei, they reinterpret the warm memories of Taiwan's local culture with exquisite French culinary techniques.

01

精緻開胃小點

Amuse-Bouche

饗宴的第一樂章。精緻的木製承架上呈現三款一口小點：Ceviche 金桔白魽塔，點綴上蘋果丁及辣椒凍； 芋頭粿，中間放上Tartar Sauce，上方為魚子醬及麵包丁； 雞肝夾心脆片，點綴青芒果。

The first movement of the feast. Three bite-sized treats are presented on an exquisite wooden stand: Ceviche, a kumquat tart topped with diced apple and chili jelly; taro cake, with tartar sauce in the center and topped with caviar and bread cubes; and chicken liver sandwich crisps, garnished with green mango.

02

金玉滿餃

Sweet Corn Velouté, Coconut, Coffee & Duo Crystal Ravioli

湯品「金玉滿餃」為玉米湯搭配椰奶泡泡，再點綴咖啡油，Side dish 是以雙層義大利餃 Doppio 為靈感，但我們是以水晶餃皮製作，內餡為奶油蘑菇菠菜、雞肉慕斯。 原菜單上的 Velouté 中文翻譯為天鵝絨醬，其法文原譯為「絲絨般的光滑」，因此以 Velouté 作為湯品滑順的意象。

The soup, "Golden Dumplings," is corn soup with coconut milk foam and garnished with coffee oil. The side dish is inspired by the double-layered Italian ravioli, but we made it with crystal dumpling wrappers and filled it with creamy mushroom and spinach, and chicken mousse. The original menu's Velouté translates to "velvet sauce," while its French name means "smooth as velvet," hence the name Velouté to represent the soup's smoothness.

玉米湯 | Sweet Corn Velouté 椰奶泡泡 | Coconut 咖啡油 | Coffee 薄皮雙餃 | Handcrafted Ravioli
03

「迷你法國麵包」及「全麥胚芽麵包」

Artisanal Sourdough & Signature Butter

新鮮烘烤出爐的歐式麵包，外皮無比香脆，內裡氣孔飽滿、濕潤軟Q。搭配塑成精緻「T」字樣的半鹽奶油，純淨的乳香與麥香相得益彰。奶油是以苗栗四方牧場的鮮奶油自製的無鹽奶油(非半鹽奶油)，另外附上來自嘉義布袋的鹽之花。

Freshly baked artisanal sourdough with a golden crisp crust and an airy, moist, chewy interior. Served with signature semi-salted butter shaped into the letter "T," creating a delightful harmony of wheat and dairy aromas.The cream is made from fresh cream from Miaoli Sifang Ranch, using unsalted cream (not semi-salted cream), and is served with fleur de sel from Budai, Chiayi.

低溫發酵歐式麵包 | Artisanal Sourdough T字特製半鹽奶油 | Signature "T" Butter
04

代表作「貝露知筍」

Hokkaido Scallop with Bamboo Shoots, Shellfish, Avocado & Lemon Verbena

搭以北海道生食級干貝，搭配上綠竹筍丁、酪梨丁、金桔美乃滋及馬鞭草油，上方以果凍狀呈現的為 Shellfish Jelly 及椰子汁 Jelly。 裝飾的花草為牙蔥。

Made with Hokkaido-grade raw scallops, this dish is topped with diced green bamboo shoots, diced avocado, kumquat mayonnaise, and verbena oil. It's served on a jelly-like top with shellfish jelly and coconut milk jelly. Garnished with shallots.

北海道扇貝 | Hokkaido Scallop 綠竹筍 | Green Bamboo Shoot 酪梨 | Avocado 金桔美乃滋 | Kumquat Mayonnaise 馬鞭草油 | Verbena oil
05

傳奇代表作「休息站的回憶」

Pu'er Tea Free Range Organic Egg - Legendary Signature

「休息站的回覆」為主廚招牌菜。普洱茶溏心蛋搭配底部的 Fondant(方旦)地瓜、蘿蔔乾，上方為裸麥脆片，脆片上點綴酸豆、脆瓜、鹹檸檬、紅麴豆腐乳、甜豆泥，另外桌邊倒入雞湯白米粥。 「Fondant」的意思為：用奶油及雞湯煎成外脆內軟的口感。。靈感來自 Chef Kai 兒時與家人搭客運在台灣旅行，在休息站買熱呼呼的茶葉蛋的記憶。放牧紅殼蛋以普洱茶湯低溫浸泡成溏心蛋，搭配 57 號方旦地瓜煎製的 Fondant，再淋上雞湯熬煮的白米粥。

"Reply from the Rest Stop" is the chef's signature dish. It features a soft-boiled egg cooked in Pu-erh tea, served on a base of Fondant sweet potato and dried radish, topped with rye crisps garnished with tamarind, cucumber, salted lemon, red yeast rice tofu, and sweet bean paste. Chicken broth rice porridge is also served on the table. "Fondant" refers to a crispy exterior and soft interior achieved by frying the egg in butter and chicken broth.The inspiration comes from Chef Kai's childhood memories of traveling with his family on a bus in Taiwan and buying hot tea eggs at a rest stop. The free-range red-shelled eggs are soaked in Pu-erh tea broth at a low temperature to create soft-boiled eggs, paired with fondant made from No. 57 sweet potato, and then topped with white rice porridge cooked in chicken broth.

紫地瓜椪糖 | Sweet Potato Fondant 蕾絲薄餅 | Pain Dentelle 最底下是粥 | Congee
06

蘆獲至鮑

Panko Abalone "Cutlet"

靈感來自廣東菜的杏汁燉豬肺，使用南非鮑魚低溫慢煮再炸，搭配來自雲林的綠蘆筍、杏仁茶泡泡，上方點綴雞皮脆片，醬汁為 Chicken Jus.

Inspired by Cantonese cuisine, this dish features braised pork lung in almond sauce, slow-cooked and then deep-fried South African abalone, served with green asparagus from Yunlin, almond tea bubbles, and topped with crispy chicken skin. The sauce is Chicken Jus.

杏汁燉豬肺 | Stewed Pork Lung with Almond Juice 南非鮑魚 | South African abalone 綠蘆筍 | Green asparagus 杏仁茶泡泡 | Almond Tea Bubbles
07

七巧魚瓏

Pan-Seared Seasonal Fish

乾煎每日鮮魚，魚底部為梅干菜肉餅，上方為五種脆片(芹菜、蔥、韭菜、蒜苗、香菇)，旁邊小點為七菜塔，醬汁為Choron Sauce (以Béarnaise為基底，加入紅糟及蜆製成的醬汁)

Pan-fried daily fresh fish, with a preserved vegetable and meat patty at the bottom, topped with five kinds of crispy slices (celery, scallions, chives, garlic sprouts, and shiitake mushrooms), a side dish of seven-vegetable tower, and a Choron Sauce (a sauce made with Béarnaise as a base, red yeast rice, and clams).

每日鮮魚 | Fried Fish 梅干菜肉餅 |Seven Vegetables 五種脆片 | Five kinds of crisps 七菜塔 | Seven Vegetable Tower 醬汁 | Choron Sauce
08

經典主菜：屏東振聲鴨三吃「鴨韻成章」

Main Course: Pingtung Duck in Three Ways

主廚向台灣經典烤鴨致敬的得意之作。 來自屏東振聲鴨的烤鴨胸，搭配洛神醃白玉苦瓜薄片及鴨腿肉香腸，旁邊點綴的是洛神花粉、糯米椒油及糯米椒 Purée，醬汁為 Duck Jus。 Second Service：使用來自石碇的許家手工麵線，放上酸菜及鴨腿肉、青花椒油，由服務人員桌邊倒入酸菜鴨肉湯。 鴨肉推薦5分熟。

Roasted duck breast from Zhen Sheng Duck in Pingtung is served with thin slices of pickled white bitter melon and duck leg sausage, garnished with roselle pollen, glutinous rice chili oil, and glutinous rice chili Purée, and served with Duck Jus sauce. Second Service: Handmade noodles from Xu Family in Shiding are served with pickled cabbage, duck leg meat, and Sichuan peppercorn oil. The pickled cabbage and duck soup is poured in by the server at the table. The duck is recommended to be cooked medium-rare.

第一吃：香煎鴨肉佐酥塔與莓果風味醬 | Course 1: Roasted Duck & Bitter Melon

金黃香脆的烤鴨皮，是無比粉嫩多汁的熟成鴨胸肉；苦瓜與醬汁; 酥脆塔殼中填入以香草、蔬菜碎、脆粒及細緻粉末組成的餡料,將酥脆、柔嫩、酸香與草本氣息巧妙結合。

Golden, crispy roasted duck skin; Amazingly tender and juicy ("tender" beautifully captures the soft, light-pink quality of well-cooked duck); Aged duck breast; Bitter melon and sauce

第二吃：芝麻脆皮鴨胸與鴨肉卷 | Course 2: Sesame Duck Breast & Roll

芝麻煮鴨皮下，是無比粉嫩多汁的熟成鴨胸肉；搭配鹹香的手工鴨肉丸卷與精美的洛神花苦瓜玫瑰。

Crispy golden sesame skin over tender aged duck breast, paired with handcrafted savory duck roll and pickled hibiscus bitter melon rose.

第三吃：鴨油酸菜細粉湯 | Course 3: Duck Oil Sauerkraut Vermicelli Soup

第三吃：清亮而濃郁的鵝澄清湯帶著酸菜的爽口與發酵酸香，細粉吸飽了湯汁，溫暖脾胃；麵線是來自石碇的許家手工麵線。

Rich duck broth balanced with fermented sauerkraut and silky vermicelli absorbing every bit of flavor.

09

前甜點「仙姿玉質」

Pre-Dessert: Xian Zi Yu Zhi

冰涼爽口的台式風味甜品重塑。底層是細嫩帶微辛的薑汁奶酪（薑汁撞奶），鋪上晶瑩剔透的蜂蜜檸檬冰沙，中央是一顆醇厚的仙草雪酪，最後托起一薄片香甜的黑糖蛋白霜，宛如仙子般優雅純淨。

A refreshing reimagining of classic Taiwanese dessert. Layered with ginger milk curd at the base, topped with honey lemon granita, grass jelly sorbet, and a delicate brown sugar meringue disc.

10

主甜點「鳳聲茶起」

Main Dessert: Feng Sheng Cha Qi

包種茶的醬汁無鮮奶油成份，以白巧克力做調和，呈現較濃稠滑順的質地。中間的冰品是鳳梨 Sorbet，桃紅色的部分為紅龍果慕斯，上方為鳳梨脆片。

The sauce for the Baozhong tea contains no whipped cream; it's made with white chocolate, resulting in a thicker, smoother texture. The central ice cream is pineapple sorbet, with a pinkish-red portion of dragon fruit mousse and topped with pineapple crisps.

11

茶點與茗心「飴意 / 茗心」

Mignardises Cart & Tea/Coffee

饗宴的優雅句點。態芮專屬的甜點推車緩緩駛至桌邊，承載著各式融合了法式精緻與台式童趣的手工小點，任由顧客挑選。再搭配一杯溫熱的精品茶或咖啡，以及清新芬芳的手工雪酪。

An elegant conclusion. The custom mignardises cart presents handcrafted treats blending French finesse with playful Taiwanese childhood nostalgia, served alongside premium tea or coffee and red guava sorbet.

Meet Chef Kai Ho (何順凱)

「以法式烹調技法，解構台灣靈魂與風土。」

"Deconstructing Taiwanese soul and terroir through French culinary techniques."

主廚何順凱（Kai Ho）以敏銳的直覺與豐富的在地情感，將台灣傳統小吃、辦桌文化與家常菜記憶，利用當代法式精準技法重組。在他的帶領下，Taïrroir 態芮於 2023 年榮獲米其林三星肯定，成為全球首家獲得三星殊榮的現代台灣料理餐廳。

Chef Kai Ho leverages sharp intuition and local passion to deconstruct Taiwanese night market snacks, banquet dishes, and home-cooked memories using precise French techniques. Under his leadership, Taïrroir was awarded three Michelin stars in 2023, becoming the world's first modern Taiwanese restaurant to achieve 3 Michelin stars.
