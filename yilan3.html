福成玩染工坊 Fucheng Color Studio · Natural Indigo Dyeing

宜蘭縣冬山鄉柯林村（福成社區） Fucheng Community, Kelin Village, Dongshan Township, Yilan
地圖導航 / Maps
早晨抵達恬靜的冬山福成社區，踏入綠意環抱的「福成玩染工坊」。由專業的藍染工藝師親切指導，從認識天然藍草、板藍植物發酵製靛開始，學習橡皮筋束綁、木片夾防染等傳統絞染技法。大家捲起衣袖，將純棉隨行杯套浸入藍染大缸中反覆揉捏、浸染，再經由清水洗滌與空氣氧化，原本淡綠色的布面奇蹟般轉化為層次豐富、沈穩雅致的靛藍色澤，親手創作出獨一無二的隨身藝術品。
Arriving in the peaceful community of Dongshan in the morning, we stepped into the lush, greenery-framed Fucheng Color Studio. Guided warmly by master indigo artisans, we learned about traditional dye plants such as Strobilanthes cusia and natural fermentation techniques. Using rubber bands and wooden clamps for resist-dyeing, we dipped our cotton drink sleeves repeatedly into the deep indigo vats. Through oxygenation and fresh-water rinsing, the pale greenish fabric miraculously transformed into deep, layered shades of elegant indigo—crafting a bespoke everyday treasure.

蘭陽船菜風味午宴 Lanyang River Boat Feast Luncheon

蘭陽平原水鄉河港料理傳承 Riverside Culinary Heritage of the Lanyang Plain
地圖導航 / Maps
中午移步至充滿在地風情的食肆享用「蘭陽船菜」。餐廳內部陳設著別具巧思的藍染長桌布，相互映襯早上的染布記憶。餐桌上熱騰騰地端出蘭陽平原經典的手路菜餚：香氣噴鼻的宜蘭炒麵、皮脆肉嫩的白斬土雞、鮮甜可口的時令冷盤，滿滿一桌展現昔日河運碼頭工人與船夫講究的紮實美味與宜蘭人的熱情好客。
At noon, we visited a charming local eatery to savor the authentic Lanyang Boat Feast. The dining tables were elegantly adorned with indigo table runners, echoing our morning dyeing journey. The kitchen served up hearty classics honoring former river transport boatmen: sizzling Yilan stir-fried noodles, succulent sliced free-range poached chicken, and crisp seasonal appetizers—showcasing the robust culinary heritage and warm hospitality of Yilan.

宜蘭人故事館 ＆ 阮義忠台灣故事館 Yilan Story Museum & Juan I-Jong Story House

宜蘭市舊城南路（前身為宜蘭縣議會舊址） Jiucheng S. Rd., Yilan City (Former Yilan County Council)
地圖導航 / Maps
午後漫步至宜蘭市中心的歷史核心「宜蘭人故事館」。建築原為建於民國48年的舊縣議會大樓，現已蛻變為承載城市生活記憶的人文地標。我們在此漫步參觀國際知名攝影大師「阮義忠台灣故事館」深邃有力的黑白紀實影像；庭園內保留著典雅的日式迴廊、石燈籠與波光粼粼的錦鯉水池；展廳中更典藏了難得一見的傳統手工木雕糕餅模具、紅龜印與糖塔模，每一件展品都述說著老台灣常民節慶的祈願與匠人巧思。
In the afternoon, we explored the Yilan Story Museum in the historic heart of Yilan City. Built in 1959 as the County Council Hall, this mid-century architectural landmark has been transformed into a cultural hub. We immersed ourselves in world-renowned photographer Juan I-Jong's emotive black-and-white documentary works, walked along peaceful Japanese-style corridors beside tranquil koi ponds, and marveled at rare collections of antique hand-carved pastry molds, red turtle cake stamps, and festive sugar towers depicting Taiwan's folk blessings.

竹林松杉靈芝農場 Zhulin Songshan Lingzhi Mushroom Farm

宜蘭縣員山鄉（有機靈芝培育產銷班） Yuanshan Township, Yilan County (Organic Lingzhi Cultivation)
地圖導航 / Maps
午後陽光斜照，我們來到坐落於員山鄉田間的「竹林松杉靈芝農場」。農場主人親自帶領大家參觀珍貴的相思木木屑發酵堆——這是靈芝生長天然且純淨的養分基質。走進溫控溫室，一株株形體飽滿、宛如鹿角與祥雲的松杉靈芝正旺盛生長。隨後每位旅人體驗了「養生靈芝茶包 DIY」，精選切片赤靈芝、甘甜紅棗與枸杞，依照黃金比例裝入食品級不織布濾袋中，帶回滿滿的活力與健康元氣。
Under the gentle afternoon light, we visited the Zhulin Songshan Lingzhi Farm nestled in rural Yuanshan. The host guided us through mounds of naturally fermented acacia wood sawdust—the organic substrate powering Lingzhi mushroom growth. Inside climate-controlled greenhouses, magnificent Ganoderma mushrooms shaped like antlers and auspicious clouds flourished. We then crafted our own wellness tea bags, carefully portioning dried red Lingzhi slices, jujube dates, and goji berries into food-grade filter pouches to bring wholesome vitality back home.

捷絲旅宜蘭礁溪館 ＆ 合棗園晚餐 Just Sleep Jiaoxi & Hezaoyuan Banquet Dinner

宜蘭縣礁溪鄉德陽路 ‧ 碳酸氫鈉泉舒活放鬆 Deyang Rd., Jiaoxi Township · Mineral Hot Springs Soak
地圖導航 / Maps
傍晚抵達今晚下榻的精品溫泉飯店「捷絲旅宜蘭礁溪館」。庭院步道在暮色中亮起溫暖的燈串，洋溢濃濃的度假氛圍。晚間先至在地口碑名店「合棗園」品嘗豐富熱鬧的蘭陽合菜。回房後，每間客房均配置專屬的超大超深雙人原石磨石子溫泉湯池，注入無色無味的純淨碳酸氫鈉泉（俗稱美人湯），徹底洗去一日疲憊；隨後還可悠閒漫步於礁溪溫泉夜市與湯圍溝溫泉公園，享受微涼秋夜的愜意。
At dusk, we arrived at the boutique hot spring resort Just Sleep Jiaoxi. Festive string lights illuminated the garden walkways, radiating a cozy retreat ambiance. For dinner, we dined at the renowned local restaurant Hezaoyuan for a celebratory banquet of seasonal specialties. Back in our guestrooms, each featured an oversized natural terrazzo sunken tub fed with pure sodium bicarbonate mineral waters (Taiwan's famed "Beauty Spring"). Later, an evening stroll to Tangweigou Hot Spring Park capped off a restful autumn night.

豐盛愛玉休閒果園 Fengsheng Aiyu Jelly Fig Orchard

宜蘭縣員山鄉 ‧ 台灣特有種植物攀藤果園 Yuanshan Township, Yilan · Endemic Climbing Fig Orchard
地圖導航 / Maps
驅車來到員山鄉的「豐盛愛玉」。愛玉是全世界僅產於台灣的珍貴特有植物，果園中一根根整齊的水泥柱上爬滿了翠綠繁茂的愛玉藤蔓。園主熱情解說愛玉獨特的「榕小蜂授粉」奇妙共生生態，帶大家親手觸摸剛採摘、飽滿渾圓的青綠色愛玉隱花果，認識從一粒微小種子到晶瑩剔透、天然消暑愛玉凍的神奇旅程。
A scenic drive brought us to Fengsheng Aiyu Orchard in Yuanshan. The Aiyu jelly fig (Ficus pumila var. awkeotsang) is a botanical treasure endemic exclusively to Taiwan. In the orchard, vigorous emerald vines climb neat rows of concrete pillars. The orchard owner vividly demonstrated the fascinating mutualistic pollination with microscopic Aiyu fig wasps and let us touch freshly harvested syconia fruits, learning the fascinating process from tiny seed to refreshing, crystal-clear Aiyu jelly.

內城生態社區 ‧ 鐵牛力阿卡車隊 ＆ 太陽埤湧泉 Neicheng Eco-Community: Iron Ox Li-a-ka Carts & Taiyangpi Springs

宜蘭縣員山鄉內城村 ‧ 傳統耕耘機改裝觀光車巡禮 Neicheng Village, Yuanshan · Iconic Cultivator Sightseeing Tour
地圖導航 / Maps
迎著秋風，登上最具宜蘭農村代表性的「鐵牛力阿卡」！這項由老農耕耘機改裝的休閒車隊，由在地長輩熟練駕駛，引擎「噠噠噠」地帶領大家在無邊翠綠的田埂小徑間暢快前行。車隊抵達隱身於山坳中的「太陽埤」——這座由天然地下湧泉形成的靜謐湖泊，水質清澈如鏡，倒映著雪山山脈連綿的山峰與綠樹，令人心曠神怡。
Riding the crisp autumn breeze, we boarded Neicheng's signature "Iron Ox Li-a-ka" farm carts! These nostalgic sightseeing carriages, transformed from vintage diesel walking tractors and piloted by friendly village elders, chugged briskly along scenic rural ridges. The caravan stopped at "Taiyangpi"—a secluded lake fed by crystal-pure subterranean springs. Reflecting the verdant foothills of the Hsuehshan Range, the emerald waters offered serene tranquility.

宜蘭秋季慢活小叮嚀 Yilan Autumn Travel Tips

天氣與穿著 / Weather & Attire

10月下旬蘭陽平原氣候宜人、微風徐涼（約 20℃ - 26℃）。建議洋蔥式穿著，攜帶一件防風薄外套，因偶有陣雨，折傘與防滑好走的休閒鞋為必備。

In late autumn, Yilan enjoys pleasant temperatures (20°C - 26°C) and gentle breezes. Layered clothing with a lightweight windbreaker is recommended. Keep a compact umbrella and comfortable walking shoes handy for occasional coastal showers.

手作品保存 / Caring for Handicrafts

本趟行程滿載 4 項手作品：手染杯袋請陰乾定色；靈芝茶包可置於乾燥陰涼處；金桔蜜餞靜置 2-3 天入味後冷藏風味最佳！

You will take home 4 handcrafted souvenirs: air-dry your indigo cup sleeve in the shade; store Lingzhi tea bags in a dry, cool place; and let candied kumquats cure at room temperature for 2-3 days before refrigerating for peak flavor!

溫泉泡湯守則 / Hot Spring Etiquette

礁溪碳酸氫鈉泉水質無色無味，富含礦物質。泡湯前記得補充足量溫開水，每次浸泡建議 15 分鐘為宜，起身時注意防滑。

Jiaoxi's sodium bicarbonate hot springs are clear, odorless, and mineral-rich. Drink plenty of water before soaking. Limiting each dip to around 15 minutes is advised, and take care on wet surfaces when stepping out.
