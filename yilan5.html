福成玩染 ── 植物與茶的色彩密碼

Fucheng Natural Dyeing Workshop: Botanical & Tea Hues
宜蘭縣冬山鄉福成路 (Google 地圖) Dongshan Township, Yilan (Google Maps)

走進隱身於冬山茶鄉的福成社區，這裡以在地採摘的茶葉、植物與天然礦物為染材。在工藝師引領下，折疊、綑綁、浸泡至反覆氧化，純白棉麻布料在染缸中緩緩綻放深淺有致的溫潤大地色澤。親手拆開棉繩的瞬間，每件作品都是獨一無二的大自然光影印記。

Tucked away in the serene tea country of Dongshan, Fucheng Community preserves the timeless craft of natural botanical dyeing. Utilizing locally harvested tea leaves, herbs, and natural minerals, artisans guide you through folding, binding, and immersion oxidation. As you untie the bindings, reveal unique patterns imprinted with the gentle earth tones of Yilan.

蘭陽船菜 ── 尋訪老宜蘭的水路盛宴

Lanyang Riverboat Cuisine: Historic Culinary Feast
宜蘭縣五結鄉 (Google 地圖) Wujie Township, Yilan (Google Maps)

昔日宜蘭水路發達，船工與商賈在沿河航行中衍生出講究火候與滋味的「船菜」飲食文化。主廚堅持傳承老宜蘭手路，端出湯頭甘醇鮮美的經典「西魯肉」、酥脆噴香的「卜肉」與古法蒸鮮魚。每一道料理都是數十年炊煙裡淬煉的濃郁鄉土情懷。

In eras when waterways served as Yilan’s lifelines, boatmen and merchants developed "Riverboat Cuisine" — hearty, flavor-packed dishes slow-cooked onboard or at bustling river wharves. Experience culinary heritage through authentic Silu pork stew, crispy Bu-Rou pork fritters, and steamed catch-of-the-day infused with vintage home-style comfort.

阮義忠台灣故事館 ── 光影凝結的歲月溫度

Juan I-Jong Taiwan Story House: Frozen Memories & Human Warmth
宜蘭市復興路 (Google 地圖) Yilan City (Google Maps)

坐落於宜蘭市區綠樹掩映的日治官舍群，這座人文空間由國際知名紀實攝影大師阮義忠創立。展館以深刻的黑白影像，記錄半世紀以來台灣鄉村與常民生活的質樸笑靨。每季策劃不同主題展覽，讓觀者在光影交疊間，讀懂台灣最深層的土地記憶與人文情懷。

Housed in converted heritage dormitories shaded by century-old trees in downtown Yilan, this cultural haven was founded by master documentary photographer Juan I-Jong. Through emotive black-and-white photography capturing half a century of everyday rural life, the exhibitions celebrate human warmth, ancestral wisdom, and timeless Taiwanese stories.

靈芝菇菇農場 ── 科技環保與生技仙草探索

Reishi Mushroom Eco Farm: Biotech & Fungi Heritage
宜蘭縣礁溪鄉 (Google 地圖) Jiaoxi Township, Yilan (Google Maps)

Step into state-of-the-art climate-controlled greenhouses where pristine Snow Mountain spring water nurtures prized antler-shaped and red reishi mushrooms. Learn about the lifecycle of edible fungi, explore organic spore propagation, and enjoy tastings of soothing, bittersweet reishi herbal tea and crisp mushroom snacks.

豐盛愛玉 ── 穿梭黃金果實藤蔓下的手揉晶瑩

Feng Sheng Aiyu Orchard: Forest Canopy & Silky Jelly Crafting
宜蘭縣員山鄉 (Google 地圖) Yuanshan Township, Yilan (Google Maps)

台灣特有的愛玉樹是世界上唯一產出愛玉凍的神奇植物。走進豐盛愛玉農場由綠葉藤蔓構築的清涼綠廊，農場主人親自解說愛玉小蜂傳粉的奇妙共生生態。遊客挽起袖子，用純淨天然礦泉親手揉搓愛玉子，看著果膠逐漸凝聚成琥珀般通透的消暑愛玉凍，成就感滿滿。

Endemic to Taiwan's mountainous woodlands, the Aiyu fig is the world’s only natural source of plant-based pectin jelly. Walk beneath shady arbored trellises while farmers explain the fascinating symbiosis with microscopic aiyu wasps. Using mineral-rich spring water, hand-rub seeds to watch golden, silky, cooling jelly form before your eyes.

內城鐵牛力阿卡 ── 穿越田埂古道與清冽湧泉

Neicheng Iron Ox Cart: Farm Tractor Adventure & Crystalline Springs

Hop aboard "Iron Ox" carts — retrofitted two-wheel farm tractors — driven by charismatic elder villagers for a scenic roll through emerald rice paddies. Rhythmic engine purrs escort you past hidden bamboo groves to 19°C crystalline spring ponds, a 300-year-old sacred Bishopwood tree, and historic trails rich with pioneer lore.

Traditional Harvest Rice Feast: Farmhouse Communal Banquet
宜蘭縣員山鄉 (Google 地圖) Yuanshan Township, Yilan (Google Maps)

早期農村秋收季節，左鄰右舍互助「換工」割稻，農家女主人必會挑來滿滿一大缸熱騰騰的「割稻飯」犒勞眾人。大碗公盛著吸飽濃郁滷汁的香Q白米，鋪上厚切入味的甘醇控肉、手工菜脯煎蛋與時令鮮嫩山菜。吃的不只是滿口鹹香，更是台灣農村真摯動人的互助精神。

During bustling harvest times, Taiwanese farmers practiced "Huan Gong" — reciprocal labor helping neighbors cut and thresh rice. Farm hostesses thanked workers with bountiful bowls of "Harvest Rice": thick-cut caramel braised pork belly, preserved radish omelets, and locally harvested river greens atop fragrant rice bowls.

宜蘭縣員山鄉員山路二段172巷17號 (Google 地圖) Yuanshan Township, Yilan (Google Maps)

座落於員山沃野水圳旁，棗稻田是一棟榮獲多項建築大獎的頂級木造綠建築。農場堅持全有機耕作，園內滿植金棗樹、溫室有機蔬果與金黃稻禾。走進採光充沛的空間，木質清香撲鼻而來；點一份手作金棗生吐司與特調冷泡茶，倚窗凝望水車慢悠悠轉動，時光彷彿在此慢了下來。

Set beside crystalline irrigation canals, Zaodaotian is a national-award-winning timber eco-estate. Dedicated to certified organic stewardship, the grounds boast kumquat groves, greenhouse produce, and rice terraces. Breathe in aromatic cedarwood scents while tasting artisanal kumquat sourdough bread beside lazily turning waterwheels.

橘之鄉蜜餞形象館 ── 舌尖上的酸甜記憶與文創手作

AGRIOZ Candied Fruit Museum: Golden Kumquat Sweet Craft & Glasshouse
宜蘭市梅洲二路33號 (Google 地圖) No. 33, Meizhou 2nd Rd., Yilan City (Google Maps)

台灣第一座蜜餞主題觀光形象館，顛覆傳統蜜餞給人的重鹹印象，堅持「不添加防腐劑、色素與糖精」。漫步走入純白典雅的英式鄉村玻璃屋，宛如步入童話繪本；在工作坊親手拿切果器為金棗劃上均勻花紋，加入糖與鹽反覆搖勻封罐，把蘭陽平原最富盛名的酸甜滋味帶回家。

Taiwan’s premier candied fruit heritage museum redefines fruit preserving with a pure philosophy: zero artificial preservatives, food colorings, or sweeteners. Within sunlit white country glasshouses, marvel at whimsical giant fairytale furnishings, observe modern processing facilities, and hand-craft custom jars of candied kumquat to conclude your Yilan journey.

交通出行指南 Transportation Guide

自駕循國道5號經雪山隧道前往宜蘭約50-60分鐘；搭乘台鐵至宜蘭火車站或礁溪火車站後轉乘台灣好行公車或租車自駕，移動更為自由便捷。

Via National Freeway No. 5 through Hsuehshan Tunnel (approx. 50-60 min from Taipei). Alternatively take Taiwan Railway to Yilan or Jiaoxi Station and connect via Taiwan Tourist Shuttle bus or local rental car.

季節著裝與必備裝備 Seasonal Attire & Essentials

宜蘭秋冬氣候溫和但偶有迎風面短暫陣雨，建議攜帶輕便防風雨外套、雨具與好走的防滑健行鞋；參訪農事體驗時建議穿著深色耐磨衣物。

Autumn and winter in Yilan are pleasantly mild with occasional passing showers. Bring a lightweight windbreaker/rain jacket, umbrella, and comfortable walking shoes. Dark clothing is recommended for farm crafts.

預約與體驗叮嚀 Booking & Workshop Notes

Natural dyeing at Fucheng, Neicheng Iron Ox carts, and Harvest Rice banquets require advance preparation; booking at least one week ahead is highly recommended. AGRIOZ DIY is available on a walk-in basis.
